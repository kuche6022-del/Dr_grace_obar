# Dr. Grace Obar — Full Website

Responsive 17-page static website starter for the Purpose Driven Life & Business Coach Hub.

## Pages
Home, About Grace, Services, Burnout Recovery (REST), Rewriting the Third Act (SAGE), RISE Coaching, Student Success, Workplace & Healthcare Wellbeing, Business & Leadership Mentoring, Work With Organisations, My Approach, FAQs, Contact, Privacy Notice, Terms, Accessibility and 404.

## Deploy to Render
1. Upload the contents of this folder to your GitHub repository, keeping `index.html` at the root.
2. In Render choose **New + → Static Site** and connect the repository.
3. Set Publish Directory to `.` and leave Build Command blank.
4. Deploy.

## Before launch
Replace the photo placeholder with Grace’s approved photo; add her confirmed email and a real booking tool; verify qualifications, biography, service details and location; review legal pages; and confirm privacy, consent and safeguarding processes. No testimonials or unverified credentials have been invented. The contact CTA is a starter only and is not connected to a booking calendar.
